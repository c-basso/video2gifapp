From clip to GIF in four screens

The same screens you will see on the App Store, in the order you use them.

  1. Pick any clip

    Import a video from your library, or send one movie from Photos, Files or another app through the Share menu. Screen recordings work too.

  2. Trim to the moment

    Drag the handles on the frame strip to keep only the seconds you need. Shorter clips make smaller, faster-loading GIFs.

  3. Tune the GIF

    Open GIF settings to set frames per second, crop, loop count and encoder. The estimated file size updates before you tap Convert to GIF.

  4. Export and share

    The GIF is created on your iPhone with a progress indicator you can cancel. Then send it from the share sheet or save it to Photos.

Three steps from video to GIF

Make Video to GIF focuses on the few controls that decide how a GIF looks and how big it is: the seconds you keep, the crop, the frame rate, the loop and the encoder.

  1. 1

    Pick a video from your library, or share one movie into the app from Photos, Files or another app.

  2. 2

    Trim to the seconds you need and crop with an aspect preset. Then set FPS, loop count and encoder in GIF settings.

  3. 3

    Tap Convert to GIF, then share the file to Messages, Mail, AirDrop, Discord or Slack, or save it to Photos.

Short clips with clear motion look best. For a smaller file, trim harder, crop tighter and lower the frame rate.

Frequently asked questions

Short answers first. The full walkthrough is in the guides.

How do I make a GIF from a video on iPhone?

Open Make Video to GIF, pick the video, trim it to the seconds you want, set FPS, crop and loop count in GIF settings, and tap Convert to GIF. Then share the file or save it to Photos. Without an app, the free Video to GIF shortcut in the Shortcuts app does a basic version.

Read the full guide

Is my video uploaded anywhere?

No upload is needed. The GIF is encoded on your iPhone and the finished file goes to the iOS share sheet. Online converters, by contrast, upload the video to their server.

How do I make a GIF smaller?

Keep fewer seconds, crop to the part that moves, lower the frames per second (10 to 15 is plenty for most reactions) and pick the encoder mode that favours file size. The app shows an estimated size before you export.

Read the full guide

Can I turn a screen recording into a GIF?

Yes. A screen recording is saved as a normal video in Photos, so you can pick it, trim it to the interaction and export a GIF for a bug report, a Slack thread or a tutorial.

Read the full guide

Can the GIF loop forever or play only once?

Both. Loop count is a GIF setting: choose an infinite loop for reactions, or a fixed number of plays for a demo.

Read the full guide

Why does my GIF look grainy or blocky?

GIF can only use 256 colors per frame, so gradients and dark footage band. Try the encoder mode that favours palette detail, crop tighter and keep the clip short; very long, high-FPS clips trade quality for size.

What is the GIF size limit on Discord?

20 MB per file on a free account (since August 2026), 50 MB with Nitro Basic and 500 MB with Nitro. Trim, crop and lower the frame rate to fit.

Read the full guide

Why are GIFs not moving on my iPhone?

Check Reduce Motion in Settings > Accessibility > Motion, open the GIF full screen, and look in the Animated album. If it is not there, the file was saved as a still image or a video.

Read the full guide

How do I turn a Live Photo into a GIF?

Use the free Make GIF shortcut, or in Photos tap Share > Save as Video and convert that video to a GIF with full control over trim, crop and loop.

Read the full guide

Is Make Video to GIF free, and which devices does it run on?

The download is free with optional in-app purchases, including a subscription with a free trial. It requires iOS 18.6 or later on iPhone. There is no Android version.
