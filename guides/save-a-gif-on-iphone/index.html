How to save a GIF on iPhone and keep it animated

Updated October 21, 2026, 4 min read

Quick answer

Touch and hold the GIF and choose Save to Photos (Safari) or Save (Messages). Saved GIFs appear in Photos under Albums > Media Types > Animated and play when opened. To keep the original file for Discord, Slack or a computer, use Share > Save to Files instead. If an app only saves a still frame, it is not giving you a GIF file.

Saving a GIF on iPhone is a long-press away, but where you save it from decides whether you get the animated file or just its first frame.

Here is where each option puts the GIF and how to check you kept the animation.

GIFs you make in the app can be saved to Photos straight from the share sheet.

Photos or Files?

Photos is convenient for sending GIFs again from your iPhone. Files keeps the original .gif untouched, which is better when you will upload it from a computer, attach it to GitHub or need the exact file size.

If the saved GIF does not move

Open it full screen; grid thumbnails can stay still. If it is not in the Animated album, the app saved a still image or a video. Some sites serve looping MP4 videos that only look like GIFs; for those, save the video and make a GIF from it.

Questions people also ask

Where are GIFs saved on iPhone?

In Photos, under Albums > Media Types > Animated, and in Recents. If you used Save to Files, in the folder you picked in the Files app.

Why did my GIF save as a still picture?

The app shared a single frame or the page served a video instead of a GIF. Save the video and convert it to a GIF.

Can I save GIFs from #images in Messages?

Yes. Send or open the GIF in the conversation, touch and hold it and choose Save.
