Frame rate

What frame rate should a GIF be?

Updated October 21, 2026, 4 min read

Quick answer

Use 10–15 FPS for reactions, memes and talking heads, 8–12 FPS for screen recordings, and 20–25 FPS only for fast action like sports or games. GIF stores frame timing in hundredths of a second, so the smooth values are 10, 12.5, 16.7, 20, 25, 33 and 50 FPS; anything faster than 50 FPS is not possible, and very short delays are slowed down by browsers.

Frame rate is the setting with the most direct effect on a GIF's size: every frame is stored in full, so 24 FPS costs about twice as much as 12 FPS for the same seconds.

Most GIFs look fine at half the frame rate of the original video. The trick is knowing when they don't, and avoiding a couple of quirks in how GIF stores time.

Recommended frame rates

Start here and adjust by eye:

ContentFPSNotes
Reaction, meme, talking head10–15Faces and gestures read fine; small files
Screen recording, UI demo8–12Interfaces move in steps; drop frames freely
Pets, people moving, dance15–20Smoother limbs; trim shorter to pay for it
Sports, games, fast camera moves20–25Below this, fast motion stutters

Why GIF frame rates are odd numbers

A GIF does not store FPS. Each frame stores a delay in hundredths of a second, so only whole numbers of centiseconds are possible: 10 cs is 10 FPS, 8 cs is 12.5 FPS, 6 cs is about 16.7 FPS, 5 cs is 20 FPS, 4 cs is 25 FPS and 2 cs is 50 FPS. A "24 FPS" GIF is really 25 FPS, or alternates delays to average out.

Browsers also treat very short delays (0 or 1 hundredth of a second) as about a tenth of a second, so a GIF exported at "60 FPS" can actually play slower than one at 25 FPS.

Frame rate versus file size

Size grows roughly in proportion to the number of frames. If a GIF is too big, lowering the frame rate one step usually costs less visually than shrinking the picture, especially for faces and UI.

Tips

  • A higher source frame rate (60 FPS video) does not mean the GIF needs more than 15 FPS.
  • Slow-motion clips already stretch motion, so they look smooth at low GIF frame rates.
  • If a GIF looks fast or slow after export, the frame delays were rounded; pick a value from the list above.

Questions people also ask

Is 30 FPS good for a GIF?

It works, but GIF can store 33.3 FPS, not exactly 30, and the file is about three times bigger than at 10 FPS. Use it only for fast motion.

What is the maximum frame rate of a GIF?

50 FPS (a 2-hundredths-of-a-second delay). Shorter delays are usually slowed down by browsers.

Why does my GIF play slower than the video?

Frame delays were rounded to hundredths of a second, or a delay below 2 cs was clamped by the viewer. Use 10, 12.5, 20 or 25 FPS.
