The best GIF maker apps for iPhone, compared honestly

Updated October 21, 2026, 4 min read

Quick answer

For an occasional GIF, Apple's free Video to GIF shortcut is enough. For stickers, text and sharing to the GIPHY library, use GIPHY. When the GIF has to look right and fit a size limit, a dedicated converter such as Make Video to GIF gives you trim, crop, frame rate, loop count and encoder control with an estimated file size before export. We make Make Video to GIF, so weigh that recommendation accordingly.

"Best" depends on the job. A meme for a group chat, a 20 MB-limited Discord clip and a GIF of a bug for GitHub need different things, and no single app wins at all of them.

Below are the three kinds of tools people actually use on iPhone, judged on the questions that decide the result: can you pick the exact seconds, control size, keep the video private, and get a real GIF file out.

Pick by situation

Reaction for a group chat: the shortcut or GIPHY. A caption or sticker on top: GIPHY. Discord, Slack, a forum or a README where size and readability matter: a converter with FPS, crop and a size estimate. A screen recording of an app: a converter, because cropping the status bar and lowering FPS keeps UI text sharp and the file small.

What to avoid

Watch for apps that export a video and call it a GIF (it will not animate as an image in other apps), watermark the result unless you pay, or upload your clip to a server to convert it. If privacy matters, check that the conversion happens on the device.

Questions people also ask

Is there a free GIF maker on iPhone?

Yes. The Video to GIF shortcut in Apple's Shortcuts Gallery is free and makes basic GIFs from videos.

Which GIF app gives the smallest files?

One that lets you control frame rate, crop and encoder. File size depends far more on those settings than on the app itself.

Do GIF apps upload my videos?

Some converters do, especially websites. Shortcuts and Make Video to GIF make the GIF on the iPhone; on GIPHY, uploading to its public library is a separate, optional step.
