How to crop a GIF on iPhone

Updated October 21, 2026, 4 min read

Quick answer

Crop the video before it becomes a GIF: in Make Video to GIF choose an aspect preset (for example square) in the crop step, frame the subject, then set FPS and loop and convert. Cropping a GIF that already exists re-encodes it and loses quality, so start from the original video whenever you have it.

Phone videos are tall; chat bubbles, Discord embeds and READMEs are not. A crop puts the subject in the middle of the frame and removes the pixels nobody looks at.

Cropping is also the second-biggest file size saving after trimming, so a well-cropped GIF is both easier to read and faster to load.

Which aspect ratio to choose

Pick by where the GIF will be shown:

ShapeGood for
Square (1:1)Reactions, chat bubbles, Discord, profile-style GIFs
PortraitFull-height phone UI, stories, a person standing
WideDesktop docs, READMEs, landscape game or sports clips

Crop versus resize

Cropping removes the edges; resizing shrinks everything, including text and faces. For GIFs of interfaces or anything with text, crop instead of resizing so details stay sharp.

Tips

  • Leave a little space around a face; tight crops look cramped when the head moves.
  • For screen recordings, crop out the status bar and home indicator.
  • If the subject moves across the frame, use a wider crop rather than cutting it off mid-loop.

Questions people also ask

Can I crop a GIF without the original video?

Yes, but re-encoding a GIF loses quality. Use the original video when you can.

Does cropping make a GIF smaller?

Yes. Fewer pixels per frame means a smaller file; a square crop of a widescreen clip removes almost half of each frame.

What size should a Discord GIF be?

A square or wide crop a few hundred pixels across looks sharp in chat and stays well under the 20 MB free limit.
